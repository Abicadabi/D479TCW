Public Bus Service

Public buses serve Taniti City every day from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00.p.m. Private buses operate regularly to cover the rest of the island, connecting costal hotels with rural sightseeing spots.

Hours: 5:00 AM - 11:00 PM
Coverage Area Taniti City & Yellow Leaf Bay
Payment Options US Dollars (Cash), Local Transit Cards
Travel Tip Buses stop strictly at 11:00 PM. Arrange a taxi for late night activities.

FAQ

Things to Know

Essential facts about currency, laws, and health to make sure your trip to Taniti stays on track.

Holiday Closures

Taniti enjoys a large number of national holidays, and many tourist attractions and restaurants will be closed. Visitors should check the upcoming celebrations and plan accordingly.

Currency

Taniti's official currency is the U.S. Dollar ($). Major credit cards are accepted in hotels and restaurants, but carrying cash is recommended when shopping at small local markets and when using the bus.

Power

Power outlets on Taniti provide standard 120 V (60Hz) electricity which works with standard North American plugs. No adaptors are needed for U.S. devices.

Local Laws

The drinking age on Taniti is 18 and is not always strongly enforced.

Alcohol is not allowed to be served or sold between the hours of midnight and 9:00 AM.

Health

There is one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.

Taniti City General Hospital: +1 (684) 555-0199
Yellow Leaf Bay Medical Clinic: +1 (684) 555-0124
Merriton Landing Urgent Care: +1 (684) 555-0148

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by the older residents. Rural residents often speak a mix of the local Pidgin (which contains elements of Tanitian, English, French, Portugese, and Chinese) as well as Tanitian.
